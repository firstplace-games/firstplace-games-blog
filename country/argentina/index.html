Sports in Argentina: the most popular, ranked

The country of three World Cups, where pato has been the national sport since 1953 and Palermo hosts polo’s most prestigious open.

The most-played sport in Argentina is football: 41.3% of people who do sport regularly play it. Next come swimming (15.8%), volleyball (13.9%), basketball (9.3%), boxing (8.1%) and padel (6.9%). Quick answers about sports in Argentina

What is the most popular sport in Argentina?
Football. 41.3% of people who do sport regularly play it, ahead of swimming (15.8%). Source: ENAFyD 2023, Argentina’s national survey of physical activity and sport: 5,458 people aged 16 and over, May–June 2023.
What are the most popular sports in Argentina?
In order: 1. Football (41.3%), 2. Swimming (15.8%), 3. Volleyball (13.9%), 4. Basketball (9.3%), 5. Boxing (8.1%), 6. Padel (6.9%). The figures are the share of people who do sport regularly who play each one.
What is the national sport of Argentina?
Pato, by Decree 17,468 of 1953. By the number of people who play it, football leads.
Which major international sports events take place in Argentina?
Tennis in Buenos Aires, polo in Buenos Aires and rugby in Córdoba, San Juan and Santiago del Estero.
Can I play sports games from Argentina online?
Yes, free in the browser and without signing up: Stack, Maze, Cinco, Puzzles and Primero.

Good to know

  • Pato is the national sportDeclared Argentina’s national sport by Decree 17,468 of 16 September 1953; Law 27,368 of 2017 regulates it.
  • Three World CupsArgentina has won the FIFA World Cup three times: 1978, 1986 and 2022.
  • Polo’s great stageThe Argentine Open is played every year at the Campo Argentino de Polo in Palermo, Buenos Aires.
  • Runners-up in 2026Argentina reached the 2026 World Cup final and lost 1-0 to Spain on 19 July 2026.
