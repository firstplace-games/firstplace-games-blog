Sports in Italy

The country that invented ruzzola by rolling a wheel of cheese down the road, and that now watches tennis almost as much as football.

The most-followed sport in Italy is football: 54% of the population follow it. It is followed by tennis (40%), volleyball (27%), swimming (26%) and athletics (26%). Below you will find the numbers, the sports that hold events in Italy, the games from Italy you can play free on FirstPlace.games, and every source.
