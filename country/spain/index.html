Quick answers about sports in Spain

What is the most popular sport in Spain?
Football. 59.8% of the population follow it, ahead of tennis (28.8%). Source: Spain’s Ministry of Education, Vocational Training and Sports, Survey of Sporting Habits 2024-25: people aged 15 and over who followed each sport on TV, radio or the internet in the last year.
What are the most popular sports in Spain?
In order: 1. Football (59.8%), 2. Tennis (28.8%), 3. Basketball (18%), 4. Motor racing (13.9%), 5. Motorcycling (12.4%), 6. Cycling (12.3%). The figures are the share of the population who follow each one.
Which major international sports events take place in Spain?
Formula 1 in Madrid, MotoGP in Jerez, Barcelona, Aragón and Valencia, tennis in Madrid and padel in Madrid.
Can I play sports games from Spain online?
Yes, free in the browser and without signing up: Frontón, Stack, Maze, Cinco and Primero.
