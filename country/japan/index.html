Sports in Japan

The only big country where baseball beats football by more than two to one, and where the third favourite sport is fifteen hundred years old.

The most-followed sport in Japan is baseball: 47.5% of the population follow it. It is followed by football (21.7%), sumo (20.8%), golf (10.8%) and basketball (10.3%). Below you will find the numbers, the sports that hold events in Japan, the games from Japan you can play free on FirstPlace.games, and every source.
